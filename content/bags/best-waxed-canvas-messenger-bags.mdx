## Canvas Weight Explained

Canvas thickness is measured in ounces per square yard:
- 12oz: Light, flexible, ages beautifully (Ona, Waterfield)
- 14oz: Medium, balanced durability (Builford)
- 16oz+: Heavy, tank-like durability (Frost River, Filson)

Heavier canvas is more durable but slower to break in. Lighter canvas develops patina faster.
