The men's tote has had a moment. What started as a fashion-forward statement has become a legitimate carry category — and for good reason. A tote offers the easiest possible access to your gear: open top, one-hand grab, no zippers or buckles between you and your stuff. For commuters and daily carriers, that simplicity is addictive.

The key is finding totes built with carry-focused materials and details, not repurposed canvas grocery bags. These five deliver.

## The Picks

### 1. Bellroy Tokyo Tote — Best Overall

Bellroy's Tokyo line is designed around Asian commuter culture, where totes are a default carry format. The Tokyo Tote uses recycled woven fabric with a structured base, magnetic top closure, and a padded 15" laptop sleeve. It's the tote that made us stop reaching for a backpack.

**Specs:** 15L · 15" laptop · Recycled woven fabric · Magnetic closure · Internal organizer

**Why it wins:** The magnetic closure solves the open-top problem — your stuff stays secure without any effort. The laptop sleeve is properly padded. The structured base prevents the bag from collapsing into a puddle when you set it down.
