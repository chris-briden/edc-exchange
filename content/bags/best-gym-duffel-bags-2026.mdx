## Gym Bag Essentials Checklist

What a good gym duffel provides:
- Separate wet/dry compartments (critical for sweat management)
- Ventilated shoe pocket (prevents odor and mildew)
- Multiple organizational pockets (keys, wallet, phone)
- Water-resistant or waterproof material (handles wet clothes)
- Durable construction (gym abuse is real)
- Comfortable handles (weekend trips too)

All five bags on this list check these boxes.

## The Bottom Line

The Nike Brasilia is the budget champion—solid for casual gym visits. Under Armour and King Kong are excellent midrange options for serious gym-goers. Aer appeals to minimalists who want quality without branding excess. GORUCK is for people who demand absolute durability.

Your choice depends on frequency, intensity, and budget. Someone who goes to the gym 2x/week and wants a cheap solution: Nike. Someone training for Crossfit competition: GORUCK or King Kong. Someone who wants clean design and won't compromise on durability: Aer.
