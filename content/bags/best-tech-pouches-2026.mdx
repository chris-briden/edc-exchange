If your bag's main compartment looks like a cable graveyard, you need a tech pouch. A good organizer keeps your chargers, cables, dongles, and small electronics accessible without the tangle. A great one makes you actually enjoy packing your tech kit.

We've tested over a dozen tech pouches across six months of daily carry and travel. These six earned permanent spots in our rotation.

## What Makes a Great Tech Pouch

Before the picks, here's what separates the good from the forgettable:

**Elastic loops over mesh pockets** for cables and small items. Elastic holds things in place visually and physically. Mesh pockets let small items disappear into corners.

**A clamshell or book-style opening** so you can see everything at once. Top-zip pouches bury items at the bottom and force you to dig.

**Appropriate size** for your actual kit. Oversized pouches encourage hoarding; undersized ones create frustration. Most people need a medium pouch (roughly 9" x 6" x 3").

**Quality materials** that protect electronics. Padded interiors, water-resistant exteriors, and zippers that won't snag cables.
