A weekender duffel is the most versatile bag you'll own. Friday night trip? Duffel. Gym bag? Duffel. Overflow bag for a longer trip? Duffel. The best ones pack enough for 2-3 nights while looking good enough to carry through a hotel lobby.

We tested a dozen duffels and narrowed it to five that cover every budget and use case.

Aer's Gym Duffel 3 blurs the line between gym bag and weekender. The shoe compartment is ventilated and separate, the main compartment opens wide for easy packing, and the organization panel keeps small items accessible. 1680D Cordura exterior and YKK zippers throughout.

**Why it wins:** Perfect size for 2-3 nights. The shoe compartment means your clean clothes never touch your dirty shoes. The organization is thoughtful without being excessive. Looks equally at home at the gym or the airport.
