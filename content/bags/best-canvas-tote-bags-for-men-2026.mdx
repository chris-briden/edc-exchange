## Canvas vs. Nylon for Totes

**Canvas (L.L.Bean, Filson, Carhartt):**
- Heavy, durable fabric
- Develops patina over time
- Water-resistant (especially waxed)
- Classic aesthetic

**Nylon (Duluth, Baggu):**
- Lighter weight
- Minimal aesthetic
- More weather resistant
- Foldable and packable

## Handle Considerations

**Leather handles (Filson, Duluth, L.L.Bean):**
- Develop character over time
- More comfortable for heavy loads
- More expensive
- Require minimal maintenance

**Canvas/Cotton handles (Carhartt, Baggu):**
- Utilitarian aesthetic
- Comfortable but less refined
- Affordable
- Low maintenance

## The Bottom Line

Budget buyers and minimalists: Baggu or Carhartt. Urban professionals: L.L.Bean or Filson. Heavy-load carriers: Duluth Pack.

All five of these totes will outlast their owners with minimal care. A canvas tote is a multi-decade investment. Spend the money now, use it for 30 years.
