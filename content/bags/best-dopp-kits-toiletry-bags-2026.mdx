## Dopp Kit Essentials

A quality dopp kit provides:
- Compartmentalization (liquids separate from solids)
- Water-resistant or waterproof material
- Lightweight and packable design
- Multiple carry options
- Thoughtful organization

All five kits on this list excel at these fundamentals.

## Capacity Guidelines

**Minimalist (2-week trip):** Aer Travel Kit 2 or Peak Design Wash Pouch
**Standard (2-4 week trip):** Bellroy Dopp Kit
**Full Setup (30-day+ trip):** Filson Travel Kit

## The Bottom Line

All five dopp kits are legitimate choices. The Gravel Explorer Plus is exceptional value. The Aer and Peak Design appeal to minimalists. The Bellroy bridges price and quality. The Filson is for people who want a canvas-and-leather heirloom.

A good dopp kit lasts 10+ years. It's worth investing in one quality option rather than replacing cheap alternatives.
