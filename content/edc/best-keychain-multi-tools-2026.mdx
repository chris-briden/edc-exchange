- **Blade Material:** Stainless Steel
- **Handle Material:** Aluminum
- **Weight:** 1.2 oz
- **Size (Closed):** 2.25"
- **Price:** $20–$28

The Squirt PS4 proves that capability doesn't require bulk. The spring-loaded blades deploy smoothly, the scissors are legitimately sharp and useful, and the screwdrivers fit standard fasteners.

The aluminum handle is durable and lightweight. The spring mechanism is responsive and reliable. In testing, the Squirt opened packages, cleaned fingernails, tightened screws, and handled countless small tasks.

The scissors are the standout feature. Unlike most multi-tool scissors, these actually cut—tape, string, fabric, paper all execute cleanly. This alone justifies carrying a Squirt.

**Best For:** Anyone who wants a proven, reliable keychain tool. EDC enthusiasts who prioritize functionality over novelty.

- **Handle Material:** Stainless Steel
- **Weight:** 1.3 oz
- **Size (Closed):** 2.34"
- **Price:** $17–$24

The Gerber Dime is slightly larger than the Squirt but offers a different tool balance. The pliers are functional, though limited in leverage. The knife blade is useful for general cutting. The scissors are adequate but not as sharp as the Squirt's.

Real-world testing shows the Dime excels at fastener work. The pliers gripping power and screwdrivers satisfy for basic repairs. It's less refined than the Squirt but more rugged.
