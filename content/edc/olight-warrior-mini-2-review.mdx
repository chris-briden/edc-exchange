The Olight Warrior Mini 2 occupies a unique space: it's powerful enough for serious work yet compact enough for pocket carry. This is tactical brightness without the tactical compromises.

## Specifications

- **Maximum Brightness:** 1750 lumens (turbo mode)
- **Peak Beam Distance:** 128 meters
- **Battery Capacity:** 3500mAh rechargeable Li-ion
- **Battery Type:** Proprietary 21700
- **Runtime:** 2.5 hours (turbo), 11 hours (high)
- **Size:** 3.35" (with tail cap)
- **Weight:** 3.5 oz (with battery)
- **Materials:** Anodized aluminum
- **IP Rating:** IP68 (submersible)
- **Switch Type:** Tactical tail switch and side switch
- **Price Range:** $80–$110

## Design and Build

The Warrior Mini 2 feels purposeful. The anodized aluminum is smooth without being slippery. The overall design is intentional—nothing is oversized, nothing is undercut. This is professional-grade engineering compressed into a pocket-friendly package.

The tail switch is tactical—it responds to quick clicks and sustained pressure. The side switch provides access to brightness levels and different modes. Both switches are well-placed and responsive.

The pocket clip is robust and deep-carrying. It sits securely in pockets without shift. The overall heft (3.5 ounces with battery) commands confidence.

## Real-World Testing

**Brightness:** At 1750 lumens, the Warrior Mini 2 is bright. I tested it in various scenarios:

- **Room Illumination:** A single burst completely illuminates a 200-square-foot room. This is not subtle.
- **Outdoor Distance:** The 128-meter beam distance is real. Testing at 100 meters showed clear illumination of distant targets.
- **Up Close:** The brightness is manageable up close due to the focused beam. The hotspot doesn't create excessive glare at reading distance.

**Beam Pattern:** The Warrior Mini 2 creates a focused hotspot with useful spill. This is superior to pure throw lights—it provides both distance capability and nearby illumination.

**Tactical Switch:** The tail switch is responsive. Momentary activation (click and hold) is intuitive. The switch works through leather, gloves, and in challenging conditions. This is genuine tactical design.

**Side Switch:** The side switch provides mode selection—Low, Medium, High, Turbo, and strobe. Accessing different modes is quick. The interface is intuitive after the first few deployments.

**Runtime:** In testing:
- **Turbo (1750 lumens):** 2.5 hours as claimed
- **High (800 lumens):** 11 hours as claimed
- **Medium (300 lumens):** 24+ hours of runtime

The battery capacity is respectable. The proprietary 21700 offers higher capacity than standard 18650 cells.

**Charging:** USB-C charging is convenient. The light charges to full in approximately 2 hours. The charging port is sealed properly—IP68 is maintained.

## Thermal Management

Sustained turbo mode generates heat. After 5 minutes of continuous operation, the aluminum barrel is warm. This is acceptable for a 1750-lumen light. The design appropriately distributes heat.

The light automatically steps down from turbo after 5 minutes to prevent thermal damage. This is smart—you get maximum brightness when you need it, then it protects itself.

## Battery Performance

The proprietary 21700 is optimized for the Warrior Mini 2. Real-world testing shows consistency across multiple charge cycles. After 50+ charge cycles, performance remains unchanged.

The battery indicator LED shows charge status. This is a minor but useful feature that prevents surprises.

## Water and Durability Testing

IP68 rating is legitimate. I submerged the Warrior Mini 2 to 2 meters for 5 minutes. No water entry. The seals are professional-grade.

Drop testing from 3 feet onto concrete showed no damage. The anodized aluminum body absorbed the impact without deformation. The internal components remained intact.

## Drawbacks

**Proprietary Battery:** The 21700 is proprietary to Olight. You can't use standard rechargeable batteries. This limits flexibility but ensures optimal performance and reliability.

**Price:** At $80–$110, the Warrior Mini 2 isn't budget. You're paying for engineering and materials.

**Size:** At 3.35 inches, it's larger than true keychain lights. For pocket carry, it's appropriate. For keychain attachment, it's substantial.

**Brightness Might Be Excessive:** For general EDC, 1750 lumens is overkill. This light is designed for tactical scenarios or situations requiring serious illumination. For reading or room illumination, the maximum brightness is often unnecessary.

## Comparison Context

Versus the Nitecore MH25S (a comparable light), the Warrior Mini 2 is slightly brighter and more compact. Versus the Streamlight Protac HL-X, the Warrior Mini 2 is much more compact but uses proprietary batteries.

The Warrior Mini 2 is for users who understand tactical flashlights and want serious brightness in a pocketable package.

## Maintenance and Longevity

Routine maintenance is minimal. Occasional lens cleaning prevents dust accumulation. The anodized aluminum doesn't require special care. The proprietary battery should provide 300+ charge cycles.

Olight's warranty is solid. Replacement parts are available. This light should serve for years.

## Who Should Buy This?

**EDC Enthusiasts:** If you want serious flashlight capability in your pocket, the Warrior Mini 2 delivers.

**Outdoor Enthusiasts:** For camping, hiking, or field work, the brightness and runtime make this excellent.

**Tactical Users:** The switch design and brightness serve tactical scenarios effectively.

**Casual Users:** If you just want a flashlight for reading and occasional illumination, this is oversized. Pick something smaller and less expensive.

## Verdict

The Olight Warrior Mini 2 is an exceptional tactical flashlight. The brightness is serious, the engineering is excellent, and the overall package makes sense for users who understand tactical lighting.

It's not perfect—the proprietary battery limits flexibility, the price is substantial, and the maximum brightness is excessive for general EDC. But if you want a light that excels in demanding scenarios while remaining pocket-friendly, the Warrior Mini 2 delivers.

This is engineering that prioritizes performance. Olight designed this light for people who actually use flashlights, not for casual novelty.

**Rating: 8.5/10**

The Warrior Mini 2 is tactical brightness done right. Powerful, refined, and genuinely capable.
