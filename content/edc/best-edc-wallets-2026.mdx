The bulging bifold is dead. If you're still sitting on a 3-inch stack of leather stuffed with receipts and loyalty cards from 2019, it's time for an upgrade. Modern slim wallets carry what you actually need — a few cards, some cash, maybe an AirTag — without destroying your pocket line or your back.

We tested 15 slim wallets over three months. These six earned our recommendation.

## What Makes a Great EDC Wallet

**Capacity sweet spot.** Most people carry 4-8 cards daily. A wallet that holds 4 feels restrictive; one that holds 15 defeats the purpose of going slim. We looked for wallets that comfortably hold 6-10 cards plus some cash.

**Material durability.** A wallet gets handled more than almost any other EDC item. Cheap leather cracks, bad stitching unravels, and flimsy metal bends. We wanted materials that look better with age, not worse.

**Pocket profile.** The whole point is slim carry. We measured thickness with a standard 6-card loadout and rejected anything over 15mm.

## The Picks

### 1. Ridge Wallet — Best Overall

The wallet that started the slim wallet revolution and still leads it. Aluminum or titanium plates with an elastic band system that expands as you add cards. RFID blocking built in. The Ridge holds 1-12 cards and has an integrated money clip or cash strap.

**Specs:** 3.4" x 2.1" x 0.24" (empty) · 2 oz (aluminum) · 1-12 cards

**Why it wins:** The elastic band system is brilliant — it holds 3 cards as securely as 10, and the aluminum plates protect everything. After three months of daily carry, our test unit looks barely used.

**The catch:** Cards can be fiddly to access one-handed. The push-card-up system takes practice. At $95+ it's premium-priced for what's essentially two plates and a rubber band.
