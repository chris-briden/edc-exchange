## Specifications

- **Body Material:** Titanium (Grade 5)
- **Mechanism:** Bolt action (mechanical)
- **Finish Options:** Polished, Stonewashed, Bead-blasted
- **Ink Compatibility:** Parker-style refills
- **Length (Extended):** 5.5"

- **Diameter:** 0.42"

- **Price Range:** $140–$180

## Design Philosophy

Tactile Turn approached pen design from a mechanical engineer's perspective. Every component has purpose. The bolt action mechanism is the standout—it's smooth, satisfying, and engineered to exacting tolerances.

The titanium construction is not compromise material—it's chosen because it's superior. Titanium resists corrosion, maintains surface finish, and develops patina over years of use. This is a pen designed to be carried for decades.

## Real-World Testing

**Writing Characteristics:** The Bolt Action accepts Parker-style refills, which means quality ink options. I tested with Fisher Space Pen and standard ballpoint refills. Both deployed smoothly and wrote reliably.

The pen's weight and balance are excellent. At 0.45 ounces, it's light enough for extended writing without fatigue. The barrel diameter accommodates various grip styles. The clip sits securely without excessive tension.

**Bolt Action Mechanism:** This deserves special attention because it's the design centerpiece.

The action is buttery smooth. Pulling back the bolt retracts the ink, pushing forward extends it. The mechanism requires deliberate pressure—you won't accidentally activate it in your pocket. But once you understand the action, deployment becomes intuitive.

After weeks of daily use—hundreds of opening and closing cycles—the action remains perfectly smooth. No wear, no loosening, no degradation. This is mechanical engineering done correctly.

**Durability:** The titanium withstood drops from waist height onto hardwood. No dents. No significant marks. The surface finish developed minor scratches (which is expected and part of the charm).

The clip is robust. It survived pocket carry with keys and tools without bending. The overall construction suggests a tool that will outlast its owner.

**Ink Compatibility:** The Parker-style refill compatibility is practical. Dozens of quality refills work. You're not locked into proprietary supplies.
