Finding a quality EDC knife under $100 used to mean serious compromises. Not anymore. The sub-$100 market has exploded with options that rival knives twice their price — you just need to know where to look.

We spent three months testing 12 of the most popular budget EDC knives, evaluating everything from blade steel and edge retention to ergonomics and pocket carry. Here are our top picks.

## What Makes a Great Budget EDC Knife?

Before we get into specific picks, here's what we prioritized in testing:

**Blade steel matters more than anything.** At this price point, you're looking at steels like S35VN, 14C28N, D2, and 154CM. They all perform differently — some hold an edge longer, some are easier to sharpen, some resist corrosion better.

**Ergonomics are non-negotiable.** A knife you don't enjoy carrying is a knife you'll leave at home. We paid close attention to how each knife feels in hand, in pocket, and during extended use.

**Build quality tells you about longevity.** Blade centering, lockup, action smoothness — these details separate a knife you'll carry for years from one that falls apart in months.

## How We Tested

Each knife was carried daily for at least one week. We used them for typical EDC tasks: opening packages, breaking down cardboard, cutting rope, food prep, and general utility. We also tested edge retention by standardized cardboard cutting (CATRA-style) and measured sharpness before and after with a BESS tester.
