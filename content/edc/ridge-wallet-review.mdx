The Ridge Wallet made minimalist wallets mainstream. It's proof that you can carry essential cards and cash without traditional bulk.

## Specifications

- **Materials:** Titanium or carbon fiber front, genuine leather back
- **RFID Blocking:** Yes (aluminum shield layer)
- **Card Capacity:** 12 cards
- **Cash Capacity:** Fold or roll bills
- **Dimensions:** 4.5" x 2.5" (closed)
- **Weight:** 1.8 oz (with cards)
- **Colors:** Multiple leather options
- **Price Range:** $85–$125

## Design Philosophy

The Ridge approach is radical minimalism: ditch the traditional bifold structure. Instead, cards stack between two plates. Cash is stored separately. The result is a wallet thinner than a standard deck of cards.

This design philosophy works because it rejects traditional assumptions about wallets. You don't need separate card slots. You don't need complex internal architecture. You need to carry cards and cash reliably.

## Real-World Testing

**Carrying Experience:** After a month of daily carry with the Ridge, the minimalism is genuinely liberating. The wallet disappears in a front pocket. Back pocket carry is dramatically more comfortable than traditional bifolds.

**Card Access:** The stacked card design requires slightly different access patterns than traditional wallets. You can't fan cards like a traditional billfold. Instead, you slide out the stack. After two days, this became intuitive.

**Cash Storage:** Holding cash between the leather and plates is unconventional. Bills fold or roll. This works fine for typical cash amounts (20–100 dollars). Extensive cash carrying might be awkward, but that's not the Ridge's design purpose.

**RFID Blocking:** The aluminum shield layer is present and functional. I tested RFID blocking with a credit card scanner. The shield prevented unauthorized reading at normal proximity. This is legitimate protection, not marketing theater.

**Build Quality:** The materials are quality. Titanium is noticeably premium. The leather back develops character from use. Stitching is excellent. The overall construction suggests years of reliable service.

**Card Wear:** After extensive card insertion and removal, the leather back shows minor creasing but no significant damage. The plates protect cards from bending during pocket carry—a genuine advantage over traditional wallets.

## Material Comparison

**Titanium vs Carbon Fiber:**

I tested both materials. The titanium is noticeably more rigid and premium-feeling. The carbon fiber is lighter and offers aesthetic appeal. The functional difference is minimal—both work excellently.

Choose titanium for durability and premium feel. Choose carbon fiber for weight savings and modern aesthetics.

**Leather Quality:**

The leather back is genuine and quality. It's not exotic, but it's appropriate for the price point. The leather develops patina—it improves with age rather than degrades.

## Drawbacks

**Card Capacity Limitations:** 12 cards is practical for most users but limiting for people carrying numerous cards. If you carry 15+ cards, the Ridge becomes problematic.

**Cash Handling:** Carrying significant cash (200+ dollars) is awkward. The design isn't optimized for extensive cash storage. If you regularly carry large amounts, traditional wallets might be better.

**Minimal Compartments:** There's no change pocket, no receipt compartment, no ID window. Everything is simplified. This works for minimalists but frustrates people who like organization options.

**Price:** At $85–$125, the Ridge is expensive. You're paying for design, materials, and brand reputation.

## Comparison Context

Versus the Bellroy Hide & Seek, the Ridge is slimmer and more minimalist. Versus traditional bifolds, the Ridge is dramatically thinner and lighter.

The Ridge represents a specific design philosophy. It's not better than traditional wallets for everyone—it's better for people who appreciate minimalism.

## Maintenance and Longevity

The titanium or carbon fiber requires no maintenance. The leather benefits from occasional conditioning. Otherwise, the Ridge requires minimal care.

The company offers replacement leather if the back wears excessively. This suggests confidence in the design.

After extensive use, the Ridge should last years. The materials are durable. The construction is solid.

## Who Should Buy This?

**Minimalist EDC Enthusiasts:** This is the wallet for people who understand and embrace minimalism.

**Front Pocket Carriers:** For people refusing back pocket wallets, the Ridge is ideal. It's thin enough not to create noticeable bulk.

**Moderate Card Carriers:** For people carrying 8–12 cards, the capacity works perfectly.

**Tech-Conscious Users:** RFID protection appeals to people concerned about card security.

**Traditional Wallet Users:** If you're attached to compartments and organization, the Ridge might feel too minimal.

**Excessive Card Carriers:** If you carry 15+ cards, the Ridge's capacity is limiting.

## Aesthetic Assessment

The Ridge is refined without being ostentatious. The titanium or carbon fiber front shows material quality. The leather back is understated. The overall presentation is professional and minimalist.

This wallet improves with use. The leather develops character. The overall appearance becomes progressively more personal.

## Verdict

The Ridge Wallet is an excellent minimalist wallet for people who understand and appreciate minimalism. The RFID protection is genuine, the materials are quality, and the overall design makes complete sense for people pursuing pocket-friendly carry.

It's not perfect—the price is steep, card capacity is limited, and the design philosophy isn't for everyone. But if you want a wallet that you'll deploy with confidence, carry comfortably, and appreciate for years, the Ridge is worth serious consideration.

This is design excellence applied to a simple tool. That's exactly what EDC wallets should be.

**Rating: 8.0/10**

Exceptional minimalist wallet with genuine RFID protection. Worth the premium if you embrace the minimalist philosophy.
