Unlike specialized luggage trackers, AirTags integrate seamlessly into the Apple ecosystem and tap into crowdsourced location data that's surprisingly accurate across continents.

**Specs:** Dimensions 39mm x 39mm x 8mm. Weighs 11g each. CR2032 battery (replaceable, ~1 year life). Water resistant IP67. Works with Find My app across iOS ecosystem.

**Why AirTags for Travel:** You're not buying a GPS tracker; you're buying insurance on items you're terrified of losing. The distributed Find My network means you'll get location data even in remote areas where dedicated trackers have no coverage. The simplicity is genuine—open, pair, add to Find My. No subscriptions, no separate apps, no complex setup. Apple's tight ecosystem integration means notifications arrive reliably.
