A dedicated tech kit isn't luxury—it's insurance against connectivity chaos and the frustration of hunting for that one adapter at an airport gate. These five systems range from minimalist cable organizers to comprehensive tech pouches, each engineered to keep your digital life sorted through any travel scenario.
