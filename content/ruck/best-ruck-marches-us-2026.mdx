Ruck march events combine distance, load, and community. Unlike pure fitness challenges, ruck events are often structured challenges with defined routes and specific load requirements.

Here are the best US ruck march events for 2026, with dates, distances, and what to expect.

## 1. Bataan Memorial Death March (BMDM)

**Location:** Fort Greely, Alaska

**Date:** March 21-22, 2026

**Distance:** 14.2 miles

**Load:** 35 lbs (official), though many add more

**Terrain:** Mountain roads, high altitude (6,000+ ft)

**Description:** The most storied ruck march in America. Commemorates the 1942 Bataan Death March. Extremely challenging: 14 miles, mountainous terrain, high altitude, cold weather.

**Participants:** 5,000-7,000 people, military, first-responders, and civilians

**Registration:** Open to all. First-come, first-served. Sells out fast.

**Website:** https://www.bataan-memorial-march.com/

**What to know:**
- Elevation gain is real (not flat road marching)
- March timing is early spring (can be cold, potentially snowy)
- Atmosphere is commemorative and serious
- Support is excellent (water, aid stations)
- Finish medal is meaningful

**Difficulty rating:** 9/10 (high altitude, terrain, distance)

---

## 2. Norwegian Ruck March

**Location:** Fort Sam Houston, Texas

**Date:** April 12, 2026

**Distance:** 17 miles

**Load:** 25 lbs (light standard)

**Terrain:** Mix of roads and trails

**Description:** Fewer participants than BMDM, but equally serious. Celebrating Norwegian military traditions. Flatter than Bataan but longer.

**Participants:** 1,500-2,500 people

**Registration:** https://www.norwegianusmarch.com/

**What to know:**
- Longer distance, lighter load (paradoxically tougher for some)
- Spring weather in Texas (warm but manageable)
- Less crowded than Bataan
- Strong military/first-responder presence
- Supportive community atmosphere

**Difficulty rating:** 8/10 (distance is the main challenge)

---

## 3. Army Ten-Miler

**Location:** Washington, DC

**Date:** October 11, 2026

**Distance:** 10 miles

**Load:** None (this is a run/walk, not a loaded ruck)

**Terrain:** DC roads, including monument areas

**Description:** While not a traditional ruck march, the Army Ten-Miler attracts the running/rucking community. Official 10-miler, but rucking culture is strong here.

**Participants:** 30,000+ people (massive event)

**Registration:** https://www.armytenmiler.com/

**What to know:**
- Huge event with excellent infrastructure
- Held in DC (tourist-friendly location)
- October is ideal weather (fall in DC)
- No load required, but ruckers are welcome
- Post-race expo and celebrations
- Great for first-time event participation

**Difficulty rating:** 6/10 (manageable 10 miles, no load)

## 4. Marine Corps Marathon ruck Division

**Location:** Washington, DC

**Date:** October 26, 2026

**Distance:** 26.2 miles (marathon)

**Load:** Varies (typically 20-45 lbs)

**Terrain:** DC roads and trails

**Description:** Full marathon but with rucking divisions. Alternative to purely running the marathon. Tests serious endurance.

**Participants:** 20,000+ total, ruck contingent growing

**Registration:** https://www.marinemarathon.com/

**What to know:**
- Official ruck divisions (cadre-led teams)
- Full marathon distance (serious undertaking)
- October DC weather (ideal conditions)
- Massive support and crowd energy
- GORUCK often leads organized teams

**Difficulty rating:** 9/10 (marathon distance, cumulative fatigue)

## 5. GORUCK Star Course

**Location:** Multiple cities (rotating)

**Date:** Year-round, dates vary by location

**Distance:** Varies (typically 4-8 miles)

**Load:** 40-60 lbs (event-standard)

**Terrain:** Urban and surrounding areas

**Description:** GORUCK-branded official events led by cadre. Progressive difficulty (Light, Standard, Heavy). This is the rucking community's main event platform.

**Participants:** 50-300 per event depending on location and type

**Registration:** https://www.goruck.com/events/

**What to know:**
- Multiple options: Light (4 miles, 20 lbs), Standard (4-6 miles, 40-60 lbs), Heavy (8+ miles, 80+ lbs)
- Cadre-led with unknown challenges
- Quarterly calendar, check website for near you
- Most accessible for serious ruckers (specific preparation pathway)
- Team-based atmosphere

**Difficulty rating:** Light 5/10, Standard 7/10, Heavy 9/10

## 6. Murph Challenge (Various Locations)

**Location:** Nationwide (May 24, 2026)

**Distance:** No fixed distance (work-based)

**Load:** Weighted vest (20 lbs) typical, optional

**Terrain:** N/A (gym or open space)

**Description:** CrossFit-originated memorial challenge held annually. Not a traditional ruck march but attracts rucking community. Simple: 1-mile run, 100 pull-ups, 200 push-ups, 300 squats, 1-mile run. Do it weighted for ruck challenge.

**Participants:** Varies widely by location (hundreds to thousands nationwide)

**Registration:** Usually free or donation-based. Check local CrossFit boxes.

**What to know:**
- Memorial to fallen (serious undertone)
- Work-based rather than distance-based
- Can do weighted or unweighted
- Very flexible format
- Strong community atmosphere

**Difficulty rating:** 6-8/10 depending on how you approach it

## How to Choose an Event

### For First-Timers

**Start with:** GORUCK Light (4 miles, 20 lbs) or local ruck club event

**Reason:** Low-pressure, supportive atmosphere, official cadre support

**Timeline:** Build to 6-8 miles with 35-40 lbs before considering standard events

### For Intermediate Ruckers

**Try:** GORUCK Standard (6 miles, 40-60 lbs) or Murph Challenge

**Reason:** More challenging, official structure, community atmosphere

**Timeline:** Complete Light level events first, then progress

### For Advanced Ruckers

**Challenge yourself with:** Bataan, Norwegian March, or Marine Corps Marathon Ruck

**Reason:** Serious distance, high altitude, true test of fitness

**Timeline:** Months of preparation required

### For Elite Athletes

**Test limits with:** Eco Challenge, multiple Ruck Standard/Heavy events, or self-designed extreme challenges

## Event Preparation Checklist

### 4 Weeks Before

- Register (most events sell out)
- Begin dedicated training
- Research the route (if available)
- Join online communities for tips

### 2 Weeks Before

- Finalize gear
- Test all equipment on training rucks
- Download maps or route information
- Confirm registration details

### 1 Week Before

- Taper training (reduce volume)
- Eat well and hydrate
- Sleep prioritized
- Review event day logistics (parking, start time, what to bring)

### 2 Days Before

- Last light ruck (3-4 miles, easy pace)
- Rest day before event
- Prepare gear (pack everything the night before)

## Event-Day Essentials

### Mandatory

- Proper footwear (tested)
- Quality socks (not cheap ones)
- Pack/hydration system (tested)
- Weight load (if required)
- ID and phone
- Water bottle or hydration bladder
- Light snacks or energy bars

### Recommended

- Headlamp (if early start)
- Weather-appropriate gear
- Blister prevention supplies
- Pain relief (if you use it)
- Electrolyte drink mix
- Changing clothes or backup shirt

---

## Post-Event

**Recovery is important.**

- Light walking 1-2 days after
- Protein and carbs for 48 hours post-event
- Sleep prioritized
- Ice baths or hot tubs help
- Stretching and mobility work

**Then:** Sign up for the next event or do training rucks for fitness maintenance.
